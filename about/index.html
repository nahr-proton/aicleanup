About this site

AI Output Cleanup is a small, independent site. It exists because moving text out of a chat window and into a real document is more annoying than it should be.

Why it exists

The problem I kept running into

I use AI assistants for the same everyday things most people do: drafting, summarising, turning a mess of notes into a table. The answers are usually fine. Getting them out of the chat window is the part that wastes time.

Ask for a table and you get one that looks perfect on screen, then collapses into a single column of pipe characters the moment it hits Excel. Ask for a formatted document and you get asterisks around every bold phrase once it lands in Word. Copy a paragraph into an email and you carry along em dashes, curly quotes and invisible characters that were never in your own writing.

None of these are hard problems. They are just tedious, and they come up constantly. Every time I hit one I found myself either fixing it by hand or hunting for a converter that wanted an account, an upload, or a subscription for something that should take one second in a browser. So I built the tools I wanted instead.

Who runs it

One person, not a company

My name is Marcos Nähr and I build and maintain this site on my own. There is no team, no company behind it and no investors. If something is broken or a tool mangles your text, I am the person who reads the email and fixes it.

I also build Scriber, which turns a YouTube link into a clean transcript in a few seconds. It came from the same itch as this site: a job that should take one paste and one click, without an account in the way. If you end up with a transcript that needs tidying, the tools here are the obvious next step.

You can reach me at hello@aicleanup.tools, or through the contact page. I genuinely want to hear about edge cases, because the fiddly ones are what make these tools worth using: escaped pipes inside a table cell, a numbered list that should restart, a zero width space that breaks a spreadsheet import.

How it works

Your text never leaves your browser

Every tool here runs entirely as JavaScript in your own browser. When you paste text, it is processed on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. There is no server that receives your content, because there is no server involved in the processing at all. The site is a set of static files.

This is not a promise you have to take on trust. Load a tool, then disconnect from the internet, and it will keep working perfectly. You can also open your browser's developer tools and watch the network tab while you use it. Nothing goes out.

Two tools load an external library when you ask for a file: a spreadsheet library for Excel downloads and a document library for Word downloads. Those libraries are downloaded to your browser and run there. Your text is not sent to them, it is handed to code running on your own machine.

How it is paid for

Right now, it is not

There is no advertising on this site and it sets no cookies. A domain and static hosting cost very little, and for the moment I cover that myself.

The one thing I do measure is whether anyone is here at all, using a cookieless counter that cannot identify you or follow you anywhere else. I would rather know which tools are worth improving than guess. The privacy policy explains exactly what it does and does not see.

If it grows enough that the costs stop being trivial, I will most likely add modest advertising. If that happens you will notice, because a cookie notice will appear and the privacy policy will be updated before any ad loads. What will not change is the part that matters: your text is not the product, is not collected, and could not be sold even if I wanted to, because it never reaches me.

What is next

More small tools

The plan is to keep adding narrow tools that each do one job properly, rather than building a single cluttered converter. If you keep hitting a specific annoyance with AI output, tell me about it. That is where the next tool will come from.
