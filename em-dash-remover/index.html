Background

Why AI writing is full of em dashes

The em dash is the long one. It separates clauses in a sentence the way a comma or a bracket does, but with more drama. Professional editors love it, which means it appears constantly in the published, heavily edited writing that language models learned from.

So the model picked up the habit, then overdid it. Where a human writer might reach for an em dash once or twice in an article, an assistant will use one in nearly every paragraph. That frequency is the tell. Readers may not be able to name what feels off about the text, but they notice the rhythm.

The same paste usually carries other invisible passengers. Curly quotes instead of straight ones. Non breaking spaces that stop lines wrapping where you expect. Zero width characters that take up no room on screen but still sit in the file, quietly breaking search, word counts and spreadsheet imports. None of it is visible, which is precisely why it survives a read through.

FAQ

Questions

Why does ChatGPT use so many em dashes?

Em dashes are common in edited, professional prose, which is heavily represented in the text these models learned from. The model picks up the habit and applies it far more often than a human writer would, so the dash becomes one of the clearest signals that a passage was machine written.

What is the difference between an em dash, an en dash and a hyphen?

A hyphen is the short one on your keyboard and joins words, as in well-known. An en dash is slightly wider and marks ranges, as in pages 10-20. An em dash is the widest and separates clauses in a sentence. Only the hyphen has its own key, which is why the other two arrive invisibly through copy and paste.

Will replacing em dashes change the meaning of my text?

No. An em dash separates clauses, and a comma does the same job with a softer break, so the sentence still reads correctly. Choosing to remove the dash entirely leaves a single space behind rather than deleting it outright, because deleting it would join the words on either side together.

What are invisible characters and why are they in my text?

Zero width spaces, soft hyphens, non breaking spaces and direction marks take up no visible room but are really there in the text. They arrive through copy and paste and can break search, word counts, CSV files and form validation. This tool strips them and converts unusual spaces into ordinary ones.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. The cleaning runs in your own browser in JavaScript. Your text is never sent to a server, never stored and never logged. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.
