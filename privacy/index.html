Privacy policy

Last updated 19 July 2026. This policy explains what happens to text you paste into the tools, what cookies are used, and what rights you have over your data.

The short version

Text you paste into any tool on this site is processed entirely in your own browser. It is never uploaded, never stored and never seen by anyone, including me.

This site carries no advertising and sets no cookies at all. Visits are counted with a privacy focused tool that stores nothing on your device and cannot identify you or follow you to any other website. Everything is described in full below.

Text you paste into the tools

Every tool on this site is written in JavaScript that runs on your own device. When you paste text into the Em Dash Remover, the Markdown Table to Excel converter or the AI Text to Word converter, that text stays in your browser's memory. It is processed there and the result is shown to you there.

This means:

  • Your text is not transmitted to this site or any other server.
  • Your text is not written to a database, a log file or any form of storage.
  • Files you download, such as .xlsx, .csv and .docx, are built on your own device.
  • Nothing you paste persists after you close or reload the page.

You can verify this yourself. Load any tool, disconnect from the internet, and it will continue to work. You can also open your browser's developer tools, watch the network tab, and see that using a tool produces no outgoing requests carrying your content.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. There is no advertising cookie, no tracking pixel, and nothing is written to your browser's storage.

The analytics described in the next section are cookieless by design, which is precisely why they were chosen. If advertising is added in future, cookies would come with it, and this policy and the site would be updated before that happens.

Analytics

To know whether anyone is actually using these tools, this site counts page views using Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy focused analytics service operated by Cloudflare, Inc., the same company that hosts this site. It is not Google Analytics and behaves very differently.

Cloudflare acts as a data processor on my behalf. It processes only what is listed below, and only to produce visitor statistics for this site.

  • It sets no cookies, uses no local storage and stores nothing on your device.
  • It does not fingerprint your browser, does not build a profile of you and cannot follow you to other websites.
  • It collects only aggregate basics: the page visited, the referring site, rough details such as browser, operating system, device type and country, and page loading performance.
  • Your IP address is not used to track you or to recognise you on a later visit.
  • If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, the visit is not counted at all.

The purpose is narrow: to see which tools people find useful and which pages are broken or ignored. There is no way for me to identify an individual visitor from it, and nothing collected is sold or shared.

Advertising

There is currently no advertising on this site. It is run at my own cost.

Advertising may be introduced later, most likely through Google AdSense. If and when that happens:

  • this policy will be updated first, with the date at the top of the page changed;
  • a cookie notice will appear, and the advertising script will not be requested at all unless you accept it;
  • declining will leave every tool fully working, because the tools do not depend on cookies.

Until then, there is nothing to consent to and no notice is shown, because showing one would imply cookies that do not exist.

Legal bases for processing

This site is operated from Austria and the General Data Protection Regulation applies. One legal basis is currently relied on:

  • Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), for the technical server logs described below, whose purpose is keeping the site running and protecting it from abuse.
  • Legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), for the cookieless analytics described above. The interest is understanding whether the tools are used and working. Because no cookies are set, nothing is stored on your device and no profile is built, this is a minimal intrusion on your privacy, and you can object at any time using the contact address below.

Consent, Article 6(1)(a), is not relied on at present. The analytics do not require it, because nothing is stored on or read from your device. It would become the basis for advertising cookies if advertising is added later.

Text you paste into the tools has no legal basis listed because no processing of it takes place on any server. It never leaves your device.

How long data is kept

Server logs held by the hosting provider are kept only for as long as needed for security and diagnostics, typically a short retention window set by the provider. Text you paste is kept for no time at all, because it is never received.

Transfers outside the EU

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc., a company based in the United States, and its visitor statistics are produced by Cloudflare Web Analytics. Cloudflare runs a global network, so requests to this site and the resulting statistics may be processed on servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States.

These transfers are covered by Cloudflare's data processing agreement, which incorporates the European Commission's standard contractual clauses, and by Cloudflare's certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

The analytics data involved is limited to what is listed in the analytics section: aggregate page and device information, with no cookies and no stored identifier for you.

No personal data is transferred for advertising purposes, because there is no advertising. The two software libraries described below are fetched from public content delivery networks, which reveals your IP address to those networks in the same way any web request does.

In practice there is very little for these rights to bite on here. Because the text you paste is never received by this site, there is no store of it to give you access to or to erase. Requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, which is the party that actually holds it.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@aicleanup.tools.

Hosting and server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like almost all web hosts, the provider may automatically record standard technical information when a page is requested, such as an IP address, the time of the request, the page requested, and the browser's user agent string. This is ordinary server logging used to keep the site running and to protect it from abuse. It is not combined with anything you type into the tools, because none of that reaches the server.

External libraries

Two tools load a software library from a public content delivery network when, and only when, you ask for a file. The Markdown Table to Excel tool loads a spreadsheet library from cdnjs to build .xlsx files, and the AI Text to Word tool loads a document library from jsDelivr to build .docx files.

Requesting a file from a content delivery network reveals your IP address to that network, as any web request does. Your pasted text is not part of that request. The library is downloaded to your browser and then runs on your device, where it is handed your text locally.

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
