Background

Why AI output needs cleaning up

Large language models are trained to write markdown. It is the format that renders nicely inside a chat window, so a table arrives as rows of pipe characters and a heading arrives with hash marks in front of it. The moment that text leaves the chat window, the formatting stops being helpful and starts being a problem.

Paste a markdown table into Excel and you get one long column of pipes. Paste a formatted answer into Word and you get literal asterisks around every bold phrase. Paste anything at all into an email and you carry along em dashes, curly quotes and zero width characters that were never in your own writing.

Each tool here fixes one of those problems and nothing else. No account, no upload, no watermark on the file you download.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your text never leaves your device and is never stored, logged or sent anywhere. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tools still work.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no signup, no login and no email required. Open a tool, paste your text and use the result.

Are these tools really free?

Yes, with no usage limits, no accounts and no upsell. There is no advertising on the site either, and no cookies. Page visits are counted with a cookieless tool that cannot identify you or follow you to other sites. The site is small enough that the running costs are easy to cover privately.

Do they work with Claude and Gemini output too?

Yes. ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and most other assistants all produce standard markdown, so the tools work the same way with output from any of them.

Do the tools work on a phone?

Yes. Every tool is built mobile first. Downloads such as .xlsx and .docx files save to your phone's downloads folder like any other file.
