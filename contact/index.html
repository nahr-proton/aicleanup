Reporting a bug

Bug reports are the most useful thing you can send, and the fiddly edge cases are the ones worth fixing. To make a report quick to act on, please include:

  • which tool you were using;
  • a small sample of the text that went wrong, ideally the smallest piece that still shows the problem;
  • what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
  • your browser and whether you were on a phone or a computer.

Only send text you are comfortable sharing. Remember that the tools never send me anything automatically, so if a tool mishandles your text, I have no way of seeing it unless you paste a sample into the email yourself. Please strip out anything confidential first, and a made up example that reproduces the same bug is just as good.

Suggesting a tool

If there is a specific annoyance you keep hitting with AI output, I would like to hear it. The tools already here came from exactly that: a repeated irritation that took more time to fix by hand than it should have. Describe the problem rather than the solution and I will work out whether it can be done entirely in the browser.

Privacy requests

For anything relating to data protection, including GDPR requests, use the same address and say so in the subject line. The privacy policy explains what data exists, which is very little: text you paste into the tools never reaches this site, so there is no store of it to access or delete.

Advertising and business

This site carries no advertising and I do not sell placements. Guest posts and paid links are not accepted, and that is not going to change, so please do not write to offer them.
